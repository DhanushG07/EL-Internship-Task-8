# EL-Internship-Task-8

Superstore Sales Dashboard

This dashboard was created as part of **Task 8** of the Data Analyst Internship at **Elevate Labs**. It provides a visual overview of sales performance across regions, product categories, and time periods using Tableau Public.

## Dataset

The dataset used is a fictional **Superstore Sales** dataset, containing information such as:
- Order Date
- Sales
- Region
- Category
- Profit
  
## Visualizations Included

1. **Line Chart – Monthly Sales Trend**  
   Shows how total sales have changed month over month, allowing us to identify seasonal peaks.

2. **Bar Chart – Sales by Region**  
   Compares sales across different regions to identify top-performing markets.

3. **Donut Chart – Sales by Category**  
   Displays the share of each product category in total sales.

4. **Interactive Filters**  
   Users can filter the dashboard by Region or Category to explore specific segment.
   
## Key Insights

- **West Region** recorded the **highest total sales**, especially strong during Q3.
- The **Technology** category consistently outperforms Furniture and Office Supplies in terms of revenue.
- **Sales peaked in November**, likely due to seasonal promotions or Black Friday deals.
- The **Central Region** had moderate sales but lower profit margins, which may need cost optimization.
